# Membra Ads Pricing

This is a starting scaffold, not a fixed public promise.

## Pricing variables

- placement type: car, window, shirt, bag, sticker, NFC tag, sign
- campaign duration
- owner quality score
- proof requirements
- location desirability
- expected scan or tap volume
- kit production cost
- shipping cost
- owner reward reserve
- platform fee

## Advertiser plans

- Starter test campaign: small local proof campaign
- Local business campaign: multi-owner local placement
- Regional campaign: larger placement network
- Enterprise or private network: custom contract

## Owner reward categories

- Wearable QR placement
- Vehicle window decal placement
- Storefront or home window placement
- NFC or QR tag placement
- Local event placement

## Membra monetization

Membra can monetize through:

- campaign setup fee
- media kit margin
- scan and tap analytics fee
- advertiser dashboard fee
- owner verification fee
- proof report fee
- fulfillment coordination fee

## Payment flow

Advertiser funds campaign.

Membra reserves fulfillment cost, owner reward, and platform fee.

Proof events unlock owner reward eligibility.

Approved proof releases reward through the payout rail.

Unapproved proof holds or returns budget according to campaign terms.
